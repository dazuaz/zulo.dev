# AI transformation positioning research

Reviewed September 6, 2026. These sites informed category research only. Their copy, customer evidence, proprietary graphics, and claims were not reused.

| Reference | Observed approach | Implication for Zulo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Botworks](https://botworksagency.com/) | Founder-led positioning, detailed operational examples, and an emphasis on employees and real work. | Make the practitioner visible and describe business problems concretely. Use Daniel’s own background and writing as evidence. |
| [Ajaia](https://ajaia.ai/) | Strategy, engineering, and workforce enablement are connected parts of the offer. Broad navigation covers many audiences and services. | Present a simpler three-part service offering that a prospective client can understand quickly. |
| [Swyft](https://beswyft.com/) | Engineering and deployment feature prominently, with implementation and ongoing administration in its services. | Explain integration, rollout, and ongoing improvement in business language. Avoid a decorative deployment terminal. |
| [Tria Strategy Partners](https://triastrategy.com/) | AI transformation positioning links strategy to execution and identifying valuable opportunities. | Show a sequence from discovery to measured improvement without unsupported speed or cost promises. |
| [Black & Rhine](https://blackandrhine.com/) | Leadership-oriented framing emphasizes AI decisions, implementation, and a useful initial conversation. | Give visitors a low-friction way to describe an operational problem before specifying a solution. |

## Original positioning

Zulo is an AI transformation partner that brings business judgment, product thinking, and engineering depth together. Daniel’s existing site identifies software engineering, technical product management, an MBA, and experience with AI systems in regulated fintech. These are the basis for the founder section; no new client or performance claims have been invented.

The homepage moves from the business outcome to three services: opportunity assessment, system implementation, and adoption. Illustrative workflows make the services tangible. A four-stage process explains deliverables. Existing articles provide a way to evaluate Daniel’s thinking.

The charcoal-and-lime identity replaces the personal portfolio’s watercolor-led opening. An original HTML/SVG diagram explains how knowledge, systems, and human judgment fit together. This is conceptual, not a product screenshot or a claim that a particular customer system exists.

## Publishing

The repository uses Astro’s Vercel server adapter and a BotID-protected contact API connected to Notion. Its output is not a Cloudflare Worker or a static export, so the Sites hosting flow cannot publish it without a hosting migration. Keep the existing deployment architecture and validate with the production build. No live deployment is part of these source changes.
