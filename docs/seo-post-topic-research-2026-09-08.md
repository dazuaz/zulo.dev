## Proposed search brief

- **Primary query hypothesis:** AI receipt processing.
- **Supporting phrases:** AI expense approval workflow; receipt processing automation; automate expense reports with AI.
- **Reader question:** How do we turn receipts into usable expense requests while preserving review and ownership?
- **Slug:** `/blog/ai-receipt-processing-expense-approval`.
- **Description:** See how AI receipt processing turns uploads into reviewable expense requests, with linked evidence, draft corrections, and a clear finance handoff.

Use the phrases naturally. Keep the existing case study focused on the delivered project; make the article a transferable guide that answers how the workflow should work.

## Article structure and original contribution

1. Follow one clearly labeled fictional receipt through capture, extraction, draft review, submission, approval, and finance follow-up.
2. Show which information comes from the document and which requires requester context. Explain how an ambiguous instruction is clarified.
3. Explain why a receipt and a transaction screenshot can refer to the same expense, how supporting files stay attached, and why matching still needs review.
4. Show a draft correction and the distinction between vendor payment, employee reimbursement, and company-card approval requests.
5. Explain the approver's decision and what finance receives afterward. Approval and payment execution are distinct events.
6. Give readers a compact workflow checklist and measurements to collect: correction frequency, preparation time, approval wait time, and requests returned by finance.

Include an original workflow diagram and a small table of ambiguous inputs and their resolution. These assets should make the implementation understandable. Recheck behavior against the application when drafting; the research used the case study and its September 6 provenance notes.

The existing showcase uses illustrated demo data. Label reused illustrations accurately. Current evidence does not support quantified savings, perfect extraction or matching, fraud detection, autonomous payment execution, accounting integration, or compliance certification. Proposed measurements are not achieved results.
